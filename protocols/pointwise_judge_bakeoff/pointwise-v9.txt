You are judging a CANDIDATE RESPONSE against a PROVISIONAL REFERENCE.

Return exactly one JSON object with these keys and no others:
correctness, coverage, rationale.
Do not emit Markdown, analysis, a preamble, or trailing text.
Do not use outside knowledge.

Compute coverage first, then correctness. Do not revise coverage after checking
the reference.

1. COVERAGE
Coverage uses only the QUESTION and CANDIDATE RESPONSE. Do not use the
REFERENCE to judge coverage.

Identify the material parts requested by the question. A part is attempted when
the candidate gives a specific response to it, even if that response is false
or unsupported.

- complete: every requested material part is attempted.
- partial: at least one requested material part is attempted and at least one
  is omitted.
- missing: no requested material part is attempted. A refusal, explicit
  non-answer, question restatement, or off-topic response is missing.

A false or reference-silent answer can be complete. A short answer to a
one-part question can be complete. Details in the reference that the question
did not request are not extra parts.

2. CORRECTNESS
Correctness uses the CANDIDATE RESPONSE and REFERENCE. Compare only material
claims that answer the question.

First, if the candidate makes no substantive attempt to answer, use wrong.
Otherwise classify with these mutually exclusive rules, in order:

- mixed: at least one useful material claim is supported and at least one
  material claim is contradicted. Use mixed even if the contradiction is the
  central claim.
- wrong: at least one material claim is contradicted and no useful material
  claim is supported.
- unknown: no material claim is contradicted, but the reference is silent about
  at least one material claim. This includes a wholly reference-silent answer
  and supported claims combined with material reference-silent claims.
- correct: every material claim is supported and none is contradicted or left
  unsettled by the reference.

Reference silence is not contradiction. An omission is a coverage issue and
never lowers correctness. Ignore only clearly incidental wording that does not
affect the answer.

3. RATIONALE
Write one concise sentence, at most 240 characters. State the decisive support,
contradiction, reference silence, or non-answer, and briefly note what requested
parts were attempted. Do not introduce new facts.

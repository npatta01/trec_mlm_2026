Field definitions:

- answer_present: The response substantively addresses the question and the

- supported_core: The evidence supports the central claim or a separable central

1. Identify whether the response actually answers the question about the

   contradiction.
4. Test evidence support for the central claim.
5. Emit the four fields only.

Incompleteness, brevity, verbosity, hedging, and peripheral evidence-silent
details do not lower a supported central claim. A wrong referent is not an
answer. No outside knowledge is allowed.
